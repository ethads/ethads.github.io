02

The findings

Five failed. This is how each would be written up.

Load time on a phone

8.4 seconds until usable on a mid-range phone on 4G. The preview of the same page, on the same phone and connection, reads 1.2. Most of the difference is a 4 MB header image and a page builder loading itself before the page.

Phone number

Present in the footer only. On a phone it is two screens down and not tappable. The preview puts it at the top and makes it a call link.

Enquiry form

A test enquiry, sent with the owner's permission, did not arrive. The form posted to an address at a hosting company the business left in 2023.

Security and hosting

Certificate expired. The browser warned before the page opened. Hosting was paid up, which is why the site was still there to audit.

What the site says

Five services listed; the business does three of them now and two it does not offer. No service area. No way to get a price.

03

The method

How a published result will be measured.

Same phone, same connection

Before and after are read on the same mid-range phone on the same 4G connection, slowest of three runs. A reading on faster hardware is not a comparison.

Enquiries counted, not estimated

The monthly count is the number of submissions the form delivered. It is not visits, not clicks, and not a projection.

Graded, every line

Every finding carries how it was evidenced: measured, corroborated, or stated. A published result never mixes them.

Published with permission, named

A result goes on this page only with the owner's written agreement and their business name. Anonymous case studies cannot be checked, so we do not publish them.
