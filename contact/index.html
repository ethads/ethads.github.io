Before you fill this in

What the call is, and what it is not.

The call is a suitability and commercial screening conversation. It is not a diagnosis, and it does not produce findings. Its purpose is to establish whether what you are seeing looks operational, commercial, or outside what we do.

If the Diagnostic does not appear appropriate, we will say so on the call and there will be nothing to pay and nothing to follow up.

What to have ready

Nothing formal. Rough figures for revenue direction, staff numbers and how work arrives are enough. If you cannot put your hands on those quickly, that is worth knowing too.
