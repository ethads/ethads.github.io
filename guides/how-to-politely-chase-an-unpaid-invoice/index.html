Guide

Chasing an unpaid invoice, politely.

The email and the text to send on the due date, then 7 and 14 days late, and what to do after that. Free to copy and change.

01

The schedule

Three reminders, then a call.

Assume the invoice was missed, not refused, until you know otherwise. The wording below starts there and firms up only a little each time.

When each reminder goes out.
WhenWhat it does
Due dateA friendly reminder, with the invoice attached again.
7 days lateAsks them to check, and offers to fix anything wrong with it.
14 days lateAsks when it will be paid, and offers to talk.
After thatStop sending, and ring them.

The reminders stop the moment it's paid or the customer replies.

02

Due date

A reminder, not a chase.

Email

Subject: Invoice [number] is due today Hi [first name], A quick reminder that invoice [number] for [job], [amount], is due today. I've attached it again with the payment details. If you've already paid, thank you, and please ignore this. [your name] [business name]

Text message

Hi [first name], [business name] here. A reminder that invoice [number] for [amount] is due today. If you've already paid, thank you.

03

7 days late

Could you check on it?

Email

Subject: Invoice [number] is now overdue Hi [first name], Invoice [number] for [amount] was due on [due date] and I can't see a payment yet. Could you check on it for me? If something's wrong with the invoice or the work, tell me and I'll put it right. If you've paid in the last day or two, thank you; it may not have reached us yet. [your name]

Text message

Hi [first name], it's [your name] from [business name]. Invoice [number] for [amount] was due on [due date]. Could you check on it? If anything's wrong with it, call me on [phone].

04

14 days late

When can you pay it?

Email

Subject: Invoice [number], 14 days overdue Hi [first name], Invoice [number] for [amount] is now 14 days overdue. Can you let me know when it will be paid? If paying it all at once is hard right now, call me on [phone] and we'll work something out. [your name]

Text message

Hi [first name], [your name] from [business name]. Invoice [number] for [amount] is now 14 days overdue. Can you let me know when you can pay it? Happy to talk it through on [phone].

05

Before each one

Check before it goes out.

Has it been paid?

Check your bank as well as your invoicing software. A payment made this morning may not show in the software yet, and a reminder to someone who has just paid is the one that annoys people.

Is your software already sending one?

Some invoicing software sends its own reminders. If yours does, switch one of them off, or the customer hears from you twice.

Is it only about the invoice?

Keep every message to the invoice, the amount and the date. Nothing personal, and nothing that reads as a threat.

06

After that

Then pick up the phone.

After the 14-day reminder, another message won't do what a conversation can. A late invoice can turn out to be a question, a cash-flow problem or a missing order number, and each of those sorts out faster on the phone.

07

Or we run it for you

The checking is the work.

Sending reminders on a schedule is the easy part; software does it. The work is checking, the morning each one is due, that the invoice is still unpaid, and then dealing with the reply. That is what the follow-up service does: a person checks each invoice before anything goes out, using wording you approved, and passes every reply on to you. After the last reminder, the invoice comes back to you for the call.
