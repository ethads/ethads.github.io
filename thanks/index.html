What happens next

Three steps, and none of them are a sales sequence.

  1. 01We reply within one business day to arrange a time. If what you have described is clearly outside what we do, we will say so in that reply rather than book a call to tell you.
  2. 02The call runs 45 minutes. We talk through how the business runs and look at high-level figures across the last three years. Nothing to prepare — rough numbers are enough.
  3. 03You get a straight answer about whether the issue looks operational, commercial, or something else. If the Diagnostic is not appropriate, that is what we will tell you.

You will not be added to a mailing list and you will not be followed up more than twice.
