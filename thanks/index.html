What happens next

Three steps, and none of them is a sales sequence.

  1. 01If you sent a website address, the audit comes back within two business days: seven checks, pass or fail, how each was evidenced, and the three findings costing you the most work. If the site passes, it says so.
  2. 02If there is something worth fixing, a working preview of your home page follows on a private link within the week, with the price in writing. You decide with both in front of you. Nothing is billed before the site is live.
  3. 03If you sent a message instead, Adam replies within one business day. If what you described is outside what we do, the reply says so rather than booking a call to tell you.

You will not be added to a mailing list and you will not be followed up more than once.
