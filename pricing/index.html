03

Founding rate

The first 5 pay less, for as long as they stay.

$249 a month instead of $349, held for as long as the site is with us. In return: a review once the site has been live a month, and permission to publish the before-and-after with your name on it. It is a trade, not a discount. It closes at 5 and does not come back.

05

Terms

What fixed means here.

Nothing before launch

The audit and the preview cost nothing. The first month is billed the day the site goes live.

No contract

Month to month. Cancel any time. The site comes down at the end of the month you have paid for.

You keep the domain and the content

The domain is registered in your name from day one and we never hold it. The photos and the words are yours whenever you leave.

The pages become yours

After 12 monthly payments the build is yours outright, at no extra cost. Want it sooner? Pay the difference between what you have paid and $4,900. Leave before then and the site comes down at the end of the month you have paid for; the domain and the content go with you.

The price is the price

What is published here is what you pay. Nothing is added at launch and nothing changes once you say go.

Fixed in writing

Before anything starts you get one page stating the price, what is included and when it is billed.

06

Not on this page

Two things we price on a call.

A larger site, a shop, or anything with a customer login is quoted after the audit, fixed in writing like everything else. An AI receptionist that answers the calls you miss, rather than texting them back, is priced on a call as well, because the right answer depends on how many calls you miss. Neither is a surprise added at launch; if it is not on this page it is not on your invoice.
