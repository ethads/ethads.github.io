04

Terms

What fixed means here.

If we miss one, that month is free

If we miss a follow-up you gave us, you do not pay for that month.

No contract

Month to month, on every plan. Cancel by email. There is no minimum term and no exit fee.

When billing starts

Follow-up is billed from the day the first message goes out. The website plans are billed from the day the site goes live. For the first five businesses, the first 30 days of follow-up are free.

What is yours from day one

Your customer list and your message history, with a copy whenever you ask. On the website plans, the domain is registered in your name. We look after it and pay its renewal while you are with us, and hand you the login whenever you ask. The photos and the words are yours whenever you leave.

Who owns the website

On Pay once the website and its setup are yours from launch day. You also get a handover pack with every part of the setup written down, so you can run it yourself or take it anywhere. Cancel the $149 a month whenever you like: the follow-up and the hosting stop, and you keep the site. On Follow-up and website the website stays ours while you pay by the month. You can buy it at any time, including when you cancel, for $2,990, the same price whenever you buy. Cancel without buying and the site comes down at the end of the month you have paid for; the domain and the content go with you.

What stays ours

While you pay by the month, the system that runs your follow-ups and the website we built. Everything the system produces for you is yours. Once you buy the website it stops being ours: the site and its setup are yours to use, change and host anywhere, for good.

What goes out, and when

Only wording you approved on day one. A quote is followed up 2, 5 and 10 days after you sent it, then left alone. An invoice is reminded on its due date, then 7 and 14 days late, then handed back to you. A reply stops it. Each one goes out only after that day's check that the quote is still open or the invoice still unpaid.

Fair use

Each plan covers up to 500 messages a month, which is more than a business this size sends. If you are heading past it, you hear from us before anything changes.

Fixed in writing

Before anything starts you get one page stating the plan, the price, what is included and when it is billed.
