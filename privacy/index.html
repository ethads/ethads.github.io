What we collect and why.

What we collect

What you type into a form on this site: your website address, name, business name, email, phone, and anything you write in the message field. The form also records where you came from if the link said so, such as a LinkedIn post. Where you go ahead with a site, the content and photos you give us and the details needed to register your domain and hosting in your name.

Why we collect it

To run the audit and send it to you, to build and show you the preview, and — if you go ahead — to launch, host and invoice the site. We do not use it for anything else.

Logins and access

A rescue can involve access to your current domain, hosting or site. Any login you give us is used only for that work, stored in a password manager, and removed when the work is done or you ask. New domain and hosting accounts are created in your name, not ours. Audit findings and before-and-after results are never published without your written agreement.

Marketing

We do not run a mailing list. Any commercial message we send identifies us and includes a working unsubscribe, honoured within five business days, as required by the Spam Act 2003 (Cth).

Storage and sharing

Form submissions are delivered by a third-party form service to a monitored inbox, and the confirmation you receive is sent by the same service. Data is stored with reputable hosting and email providers under their security controls. We share it with no one else except where required by law.

Access and correction

Email support@serviceflow360.com.auand we will provide, correct or delete your information.
