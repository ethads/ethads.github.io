03

How it works

Three steps. One of them is yours.

  1. 01 · About a minute per quote or invoice

    You tell us once

    Forward the quote or the invoice, or text a name, a number and an amount. That is the whole job on your side.

  2. 02 · In words you approved on day one

    We follow up as you

    Short, polite messages in your business's name. A quote is followed up 2, 5 and 10 days after you sent it, then left alone. An invoice is reminded on its due date, then 7 and 14 days late, then handed back to you. A reply stops it. Nothing goes out in wording you have not seen.

  3. 03 · One page, every Monday

    You see what happened

    Who replied, who paid, who still owes you. Replies are passed straight to you, so the conversation stays yours.

07

Who it suits

Built for one kind of business.

Suits

  • Trade and repair businesses with two to ten people, anywhere in Australia: plumbers, electricians, builders, painters, fencers, mechanics.
  • Owners who quote most weeks and know some of those quotes never get a second look.
  • Businesses carrying unpaid invoices they have not had time, or heart, to ask about.
  • Owners who want a price before a conversation.

Does not suit

  • Businesses that send a quote or two a month. You can follow those up yourself, and we will say so.
  • Debt collection. We send polite reminders, then hand the invoice back to you.
  • Cold outreach, bulk marketing texts or bought lists. We only write to people you have quoted or invoiced.
  • Anyone wanting recorded voicemail drops or a robot on the phone.

What exactly do you send?
Short, polite messages in your business's name, in wording you approved on day one. A quote is followed up 2, 5 and 10 days after you sent it, then left alone. An invoice is reminded on its due date, then 7 and 14 days late, then handed back to you.
