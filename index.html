Evidence

A reading is measured. An observation is corroborated: seen, and checked against a second source. The grade is on every finding so you know how much weight it carries. Where a check cannot be run, the audit says not tested. It never guesses.

About

A small Adelaide practice. One person, every audit.

Service Flow 360 is run by Adam Johnson from Adelaide. It started with a pattern he kept seeing in trade businesses: good operators losing work to worse ones whose websites simply loaded, answered the phone and said what they did.

The practice is small on purpose. Adam runs every audit and builds every preview himself, which is why the prices are published and why the audit says not tested where it could not test. The method is the same one he uses to measure how work moves through a service business: prove the finding first, then recommend.

Nothing on this site is a client result yet. When one is, it will say how it was measured.

Questions

What people ask before they send the address.

What does the free audit involve?
You send the address. We run the seven checks on the site as it is, from a mid-range phone on 4G, and send back what passed, what failed and how each was evidenced. Two business days, no call required, no obligation.
What is a working preview?
A real page, built on your content, on a private link. Not a mock-up and not a template with your logo dropped in. You open it on your phone and compare it with what you have.
What does it cost?
$349 a month inc GST, with nothing upfront and no contract. That covers the build, hosting, security, backups, small edits, missed-call text-back, review requests and a monthly enquiry report. If you would rather own it outright, it is $4,900 once, then $149 a month for hosting and care. The pricing page shows where the figure sits against published Australian prices.
When do I pay?
The day the site goes live, and monthly from then. Nothing before that. The audit and the preview are free whether or not you go ahead.
Is there a contract?
No. Cancel any time. The site comes down at the end of the month you have paid for, and the domain and your content stay in your name. After 12 monthly payments the pages are yours outright as well.
How long does it take?
The audit is back within two business days. The preview follows within a week of that if the audit found something worth fixing. The full site launches about a week after you say go, once we have the photos and details we ask for.
What if I have no website at all?
Same plan, same price, same preview first. The audit runs on your Google Business Profile or Facebook page instead of a site.
Can you handle the phone as well?
Missed-call text-back is in the plan: anyone who rings and gets no answer is texted within a minute. If calls are the real problem, ask about an AI receptionist that answers them; it is priced on a call, not on this page.
Why are your prices published?
Because a quote you have to ask for is a negotiation, and a tradesperson has better things to do. The prices on this site are the prices.
What is the founding rate?
The first 5 clients pay $249 a month for as long as they stay, in return for a review and a before-and-after we can publish. It is a trade, not a discount, and it closes at 5.
