01

Why owners call us

A busy business can still be losing work quietly.

These are observable symptoms, not a diagnosis. They are worth measuring precisely because they are difficult to judge from inside the business.

Calls and enquiries depend on who happens to answer.

The path an enquiry takes changes with who picks it up, the hour it arrives and how busy the office is that day, so no two are handled quite the same way.

Quotes are sent, but follow-up is inconsistent.

Some are chased twice, some never. Nobody records which.

Job status lives across inboxes, whiteboards and individual memory.

The information exists, in several places that disagree.

The owner cannot get a reliable answer without asking several people.

Questions about the current state of the business are answered by interruption rather than by looking.

03

How the Diagnostic works

Four stages over ten working days.

  1. Stage 01

    Suitability call

    A 45-minute discussion to understand the business, review high-level numbers and determine whether the issue appears operational, commercial, or outside our scope. Free, and it may end with us saying the Diagnostic is not appropriate.

  2. Stage 02

    Evidence collection

    Review of agreed system records, call information, workflow artefacts and operating documentation. Where a record does not exist, the gap is noted rather than filled.

  3. Stage 03

    Field observation

    Observation of how selected enquiries and jobs actually move through the business. Any test enquiries require written permission first.

  4. Stage 04

    Findings

    A presentation of what was measured, where evidence was sufficient or missing, what deserves attention first, and what options are available.

Recommendations follow measurement, not the other way around. The Diagnostic is designed to establish what is actually happening first, so that any decision about what to change is made against evidence.

07

Suitability

Who the Diagnostic is for.

It may suit businesses that

  • Employ roughly 12–40 people
  • Receive substantial inbound customer demand
  • Have several people involved between enquiry and completed work
  • Are busier than their reporting systems can clearly explain
  • Want evidence before committing to a major operational change

It is unlikely to suit

  • Sole traders
  • Very small teams without an administrative workflow
  • Businesses whose main issue is insufficient demand
  • Heavily tender-led businesses
  • Businesses seeking immediate software installation rather than diagnosis

09

Questions

Straight answers.

What happens during the 45-minute call?
We talk through how the business runs, look at high-level numbers across the last three years, and work out whether what you are seeing looks operational, commercial, or something else. There is no charge and no obligation. If the Diagnostic does not appear appropriate, we will say so on the call.
What information will you need?
For the call, high-level figures are enough — revenue direction, staff numbers, roughly how work arrives. If the Diagnostic proceeds, we agree the specific records in advance and put the list in writing before anything starts.
Will staff be disrupted?
Minimally, and never without notice. Observation is arranged around your operating day. We will tell your team what we are doing and why before we arrive, in whatever words you prefer.
Do you install software?
No. The Diagnostic measures how work moves. It does not include software installation, configuration or licensing, and it does not require you to buy any.
Do we have to use you for implementation?
No. The Diagnostic is a standalone engagement. The findings are yours, written so that you or anyone else can act on them.
What happens if you find no material issue?
That is a legitimate result and it is reported as one. If the numbers show nothing material, that is what the report will say.
Is our information confidential?
Yes. Information you provide is used only for the engagement, is handled under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), and is not shared without your written agreement. Confidentiality terms are set out before work begins.
Why does the Diagnostic cost $6,500?
It is a defined ten-working-day engagement: a structured evidence review, on-site field observation, analysis, and a written report presented in person. The price is fixed and agreed before commencement, and so is the scope.
How long does it take?
Ten working days from commencement to the findings presentation, scheduled around your operating week.
Do you work outside Adelaide?
Not at present. Current work is focused on greater Adelaide so that field observation is practical. If you are outside that area, say so and we will tell you honestly whether we can help.
